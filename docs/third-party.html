Source code of the LGPL libraries

These libraries are licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License (LGPL). They are included as separate files in the program's folder (_internal), and you may replace them with modified versions, as the LGPL and section 7 of this program's licence allow:

  • Qt 6.11.2 (LGPL-3.0-only), its source modules qtbase, qtimageformats, qtsvg: _internal\PySide6\Qt6*.dll and _internal\PySide6\plugins.
  • PySide6 6.11.2 (LGPL-3.0-only): the other files in _internal\PySide6 (its .pyd and .py files and pyside6.abi3.dll).
  • Shiboken6 6.11.2 (LGPL-3.0-only): _internal\shiboken6.
  • GEOS 3.13.1 (LGPL-2.1), as bundled with Shapely 2.1.2: _internal\Shapely.libs\geos-*.dll and geos_c-*.dll.
