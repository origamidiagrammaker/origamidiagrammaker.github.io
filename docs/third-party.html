Origami Diagram Maker

Third-party software

Origami Diagram Maker includes software made by others, each part under its own licence (section 7 of the licence). Every download comes with the full list and every licence text: THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt, next to the program. This page summarises those lists for version 1.3.0 on Windows, macOS and Linux.

Parts of the program from others

The layer-order solver and the geometry it rests on are ported from or closely follow flat-folder by Jason S. Ku (MIT licence).

  • Qt 6.11.2 (LGPL-3.0-only), its source modules qtbase, qtimageformats, qtsvg: _internal\PySide6\Qt6*.dll and _internal\PySide6\plugins.
  • PySide6 6.11.2 (LGPL-3.0-only): the other files in _internal\PySide6 (its .pyd and .py files and pyside6.abi3.dll).

  • GEOS 3.13.1 (LGPL-2.1), as bundled with Shapely 2.1.2: _internal\Shapely.libs\geos-*.dll and geos_c-*.dll.
